Jay Kuo](https://viterbi.usc.edu/directory/faculty/Kuo/Chung-Chieh){:target="_blank"} in [Media Communication Lab](https://mcl.usc.edu/){:target="_blank"}. My research interests include Large Language Models and Multimodal Learning, Image Processing and Computer Vision.
